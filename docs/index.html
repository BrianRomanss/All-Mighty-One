1Who we are

All Mighty One ("the App") is a mobile game published by HyperSpace Games Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, why, and the choices you have.

2Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

To make the game work, the App stores the following on your device only:

  • Game progress: levels, best scores, gems, unlocked games and upgrades
  • Settings: music, sound and vibration
  • Daily reward and daily mission progress
  • Whether you bought Remove Ads

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3Advertising (Unity Ads)

The App is free and supported by ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show ads, limit how often you see them and measure how they perform, Unity Ads may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size
  • IP address, and an approximate country or region worked out from it
  • How you interact with ads: views, clicks, completed videos

Unity handles this data under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalized ads instead.

🎁 Some ads are rewarded: you choose to watch them for an in-game bonus, such as a revive or extra gems. They are always optional.

4Gameplay analytics (Unity Analytics)

To understand how the games are played and make them better, the App sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies), for example:

  • Which games you open, levels started, won or lost, and run scores
  • Games unlocked, daily rewards and missions claimed
  • Ads watched (which placement, and whether a reward was given) and purchases made

Unity also records a random installation ID created by the SDK, basic device information (model, operating system, language) and your approximate country. This data is not linked to your name or contact details. Unity handles it under the same Unity privacy policy. Where consent is required, analytics only start after you agree, and you can turn them off at any time.

5In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6Your choices

  • Reset or limit ad tracking: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Change your consent: where a consent choice was shown, you can change it for ads and analytics from the App's settings.
  • Remove ads: the Remove Ads purchase stops banner and in-between-level ads. Rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings to remove everything stored on your phone. To have Unity delete the analytics collected from your device, contact us and we will send the deletion request.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. For data collected by Unity Ads and Unity Analytics, you can use these rights through Unity's privacy policy linked above, or contact us and we will help.

8Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by Unity Ads and Unity Analytics is kept and secured by Unity as described in its privacy policy.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
